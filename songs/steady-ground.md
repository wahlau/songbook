# Verse 1
When the path is hard to follow
and the evening hides the way,
You are steady ground beneath me,
You are with me night and day.

# Verse 2
When the years have turned to winter
and my hands no longer hold,
You will keep me, You will lead me,
You are faithful as of old.

# Verse 3
When at last the road is ended
and the light of morning streams,
I will stand on steady ground,
fuller than my brightest dreams.
